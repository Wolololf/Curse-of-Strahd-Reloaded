<h3>Traits</h3>
<p><strong><em>Swarm.</em></strong> The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny Strix. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.</p>
<h3>Actions</h3>
<p><strong><em>Rend.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) piercing damage if the Swarm is bloodied.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cacophony (Recharge 6).</em></strong> <em>Wisdom Saving Throw</em> DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. Failure: The target has the Deafened condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While Deafened, the target also has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.</p>
