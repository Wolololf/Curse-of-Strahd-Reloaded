Eating one pastry provides benefits the next day due to good dreams, but each day the benefits reduce:
1. +1d4 to all D20 tests, 3d6 temporary hit points
2. +1d2 to all D20 tests, 2d6 temporary hit points
3. +1 to all D20 tests, 1d6 temporary hit points
4. No more benefits

Once the character stops consuming dream pastries, they suffer from withdrawal, based on how long they have eaten the pastries. Each day of consuming pastries causes two days of withdrawal.

At the start of each day of withdrawal, make a DC 15 CON save, on success the symptoms stay the same, on failure they escalate:
1. -1 to all D20 tests, -1d6 max hit points
2. -1d2 to all D20 tests, -2d6 max hit points
3. -1d4 to all D20 tests, -3d6 max hit points
4. -1d6 to all D20 tests, -4d6 max hit points
5. -1d8 to all D20 tests, -5d6 max hit points
6. -1d10 to all D20 tests, -6d6 max hit points
7. -1d12 to all D20 tests, -7d6 max hit points

Max hit point decreases aren't cumulative. Hit point maximum cannot be decreased below 1, so characters cannot die from withdrawal.

A greater restoration spell, expensive potions, or divine intervention can cure addiction as well.
